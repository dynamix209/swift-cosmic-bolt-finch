How the vault works

1

Earn BOBUX from side-quests

Each module round includes one optional side-quest. Completing it earns your team up to 200 BOBUX, plus module points toward the competition. Skipping the side-quest — or simply doing well on the core module — does not earn any BOBUX.

2

Live events pay out too

The Scavenger Hunt and the Escape Room are the two live events — each can award your team up to 200 BOBUX, on top of anything you've earned from side-quests. The Scavenger Hunt sends your team hunting for clues and objects around the venue; the Escape Room is a timed puzzle challenge your whole team solves together. Ask a marshal what's running and when.

3

BOBUX and module points are different things

Module points decide your ranking in the competition. BOBUX is spending money for the shop. A marshal can convert BOBUX into module points at a rate of 100 BOBUX per 50 points, plus a flat 100 BOBUX fee for the transfer — a real scoring edge, but that BOBUX is gone for good, so think it through.

4

Buy, get approved, then redeem

Pick a perk in the SHOP and hit BUY. Nothing is deducted yet — your request just joins the marshals' queue. Once it's approved, the cost comes out of your balance and you'll get an email with a redemption token. Show that token to a module head to actually use the perk.

5

The shop keeps hours

Purchases are only accepted from 6:00 PM to 10:00 PM each event day. You can still browse and check your requests outside that window — you just can't buy anything until the shop reopens.
